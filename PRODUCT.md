# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Architects, interior designers, specifiers, fabricators, distributors, and design-conscious homeowners exploring premium engineered surfaces for residential and commercial spaces.

## Product Purpose

Present Kalinga Stone as a globally credible, design-led surface brand. Help visitors understand its material range, see surfaces in finished spaces, and begin a conversation with the company.

## Brand Personality

Architectural, refined, tactile. The experience should feel calm and assured while letting material photography carry the emotional weight.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS layouts, glossy luxury clichés, purple gradients, over-rounded components, excessive cards, playful motion, and interaction effects that compete with the stone imagery.

## Design Principles

- Let material imagery lead.
- Use motion to reveal, not distract.
- Keep typography precise and architectural.
- Make every action feel quiet, deliberate, and premium.
- Preserve generous space and strong editorial pacing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard focus visibility, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, descriptive image alternatives, and responsive layouts without horizontal overflow.
